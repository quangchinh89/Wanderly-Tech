Account & Data

Delete your Wanderly account

You can permanently delete your Wanderly account and its associated data at any time — from inside the app, or by request here if you no longer have the app installed.

Request account deletion

No longer have the app? Send us a deletion request and we'll take care of it — no reinstall required.

Request deletion by email
Option 1

Delete in the app

The fastest way — your account is removed right away:

  1. Open Wanderly and go to your Profile tab.
  2. Tap the Settings (gear) icon.
  3. Scroll to the bottom and tap Delete account.
  4. Confirm with “Yes, delete everything.”

Your session ends immediately and deletion begins right away.

Option 2

Request deletion without the app

If you've uninstalled Wanderly or can't sign in, email us and we'll process your deletion manually:

  • Write to support@wanderly.tech from the email address on your account, with the subject “Delete my Wanderly account.”
  • We verify ownership of the account before deleting, then confirm by email once it's done.
  • Requests are actioned within 30 days (usually much sooner).
What happens

What we delete

When your account is deleted, we permanently remove your personal data and the content you created, including:

  • Your profile — name, bio, photos, gender, home country, date of birth, languages and interests
  • Your identity-verification data (verification photos and status)
  • Content you authored — trip recaps and their photos, reviews you wrote, and photos on trips you host
  • Emergency contacts you saved and any active live-location share
  • Your login identity, so the account can never be signed into again
Retention

What we may keep, and why

To protect other travelers and meet our legal obligations, a limited set of records is retained in anonymized form (stripped of information that identifies you):

  • Records other users still depend on — such as reviews written about trips and group-trip history — kept without your name or profile
  • Data required for safety, fraud-prevention, and abuse-moderation purposes
  • Information we're legally required to retain under applicable law
Anonymized records contain no personal identifiers and cannot be linked back to you. For full details, see our Privacy Policy.
